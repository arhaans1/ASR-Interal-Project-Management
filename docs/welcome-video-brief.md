# Client welcome video: production brief (CAM)

For a single video, about 95 seconds, made with Grok Imagine (visuals), ElevenLabs (Indian English voice) and an editor (Canva or CapCut) to stitch and add text. It plays full-screen in the client portal on first login, hosted on Bunny CDN.

## 1. Practical notes before you start

- **Make the visuals text-free, add all text in the editor.** AI video tools garble words. Ask Grok Imagine for backgrounds, icons and motion only, then put titles and checklist items on top as real text.
- **Generate one clip per scene** (9 clips, 6 to 10 seconds each), then cut each to the length of its voice line.
- **Record the voice first.** Generate the nine lines in ElevenLabs, note each one's length, then fit the visuals to that.
- **The video cannot say the client's name** or change by funnel type. This script is for the Community Ascension Model. A VSL or Webinar client would need their own cut (the lines are in `docs/welcome-walkthrough-script.md`).
- Export 1920x1080, 16:9, MP4 (H.264), under 60 MB. The video is also watchable on a phone, so keep text large and centred, with a safe margin on every edge.

## 2. Colours (taken from the portal, so the video feels like the same product)

Rules: dark background always. Indigo to violet gradient for cards and lines. Emerald only for ticks, "approved" and the launch moment. No other colours. Font: Inter or Poppins, bold for titles, regular for lists.

## 3. Motion style

Clean, premium, "SaaS product explainer". Calm and confident, not flashy.

- **Look:** flat vector icons, glassy rounded cards on a dark gradient, soft indigo glow, slow floating particles in the background.
- **Movement:** cards slide up and fade in, lines draw themselves, checklist items tick one by one, a progress bar fills along the bottom of the screen.
- **Transitions:** soft push or fade-through between scenes, 0.4 seconds. No spins, no glitch effects, no shake.
- **Timing:** each on-screen item lands as the voice says it.
- **Music:** soft ambient electronic bed, very low under the voice. Fade out at the end.
- **Lower third:** small "ASR Media Pro" logo bottom-left throughout. Logo large on the first and last scene.

## 4. ElevenLabs voice settings

- Pick an Indian English voice, warm and professional, calm pace. Stability about 50%, similarity high, style low.
- Pronunciation: write "V S L" and "L M S" with spaces, write "fourteen" in words, and "WhatsApp" as one word. Keep "ASR Media Pro" as "A S R Media Pro".
- Generate each scene as its own file so you can adjust timing.

## 5. Scene-by-scene: voiceover, on-screen text, visual prompt

Total about 95 seconds. Voiceover lines are final wording, ready to paste into ElevenLabs.

### Scene 1: Welcome (about 8 s)
- **Voiceover:** "Welcome to ASR Media Pro. In the next minute and a half, here is how we will build your Community Ascension Model together."
- **On-screen text:** "Welcome to ASR Media Pro" / "Your Community Ascension Model"
- **Grok Imagine prompt:** "Dark midnight blue background with a soft indigo and violet glow, slow floating light particles, a glowing logo-shaped light bloom in the centre, cinematic, premium tech feel, no text, 16:9."

### Scene 2: The model (about 11 s)
- **Voiceover:** "Buyers start with a low-ticket course, join your community, and the right ones move up into your high-ticket programme."
- **On-screen text:** "Course → Community → High-ticket programme"
- **Grok Imagine prompt:** "Three glassy rounded cards on a dark background connected by a glowing indigo line, each one larger than the last, like a staircase going up, flat vector icons, soft glow, no text."

### Scene 3: Your onboarding (about 16 s)
- **Voiceover:** "It starts with you. Watch the short fill-up guide, open your Drive folder, and complete the onboarding worksheet. Then share your logo, photos, testimonials and case studies, and give us access to your Meta ad account, payment gateway and domain."
- **On-screen text:** "Complete your onboarding", then a checklist that ticks in as each is spoken: Watch the fill-up guide · Open your Google Drive · Fill the onboarding worksheet · Share your logo · Share photos, testimonials, case studies · Meta ad account access · Payment gateway access · Domain DNS access
- **Visual:** build the checklist in the editor (8 rows, emerald ticks). Grok Imagine only for the background: "Dark indigo gradient, soft floating particles, empty glass panel, no text."

### Scene 4: Welcome call (about 7 s)
- **Voiceover:** "Once your onboarding is complete, we meet on a welcome call. Right after that, our work begins."
- **On-screen text:** "Welcome call → We start work"
- **Grok Imagine prompt:** "Two simple flat avatar circles on a dark background, a glowing line connecting them, a small video-call window glowing indigo, friendly, minimal, no text."

### Scene 5: Research and outline (about 9 s)
- **Voiceover:** "We study your market, your audience and your competitors. Within three working days, your course outline reaches you for approval."
- **On-screen text:** "Market · Audience · Competitor research" then "Course outline: 3 working days" then "Your approval" with an emerald tick
- **Grok Imagine prompt:** "Magnifying glass sweeping over glowing data charts on a dark indigo background, charts resolving into a clean document, flat vector, no text."

### Scene 6: We build everything (about 14 s)
- **Voiceover:** "Once you approve, we build it all in five working days: your course slide decks and bonuses, the sales page and copy, your V S L and ad scripts, image ads, your community and L M S, and the full funnel."
- **On-screen text:** "We build everything · 5 working days", with seven icons popping in: Slide decks · Bonuses · Sales page · V S L and ad scripts · Image ads · Community and L M S · Funnel
- **Grok Imagine prompt:** "Grid of glowing glassy tiles assembling one by one on a dark background, each with a simple flat icon (slides, gift, web page, play button, image, chat bubbles, funnel), indigo and violet glow, no text."

### Scene 7: Your part, recording (about 10 s)
- **Voiceover:** "Your part is recording: your course, your V S L and your video ads. We give you the scripts, the slide decks, clear recording guidelines and references."
- **On-screen text:** "You record. We give you everything you need." Under it: Scripts · Slide decks · Guidelines · References
- **Grok Imagine prompt:** "A softly lit camera and microphone icon glowing indigo on a dark background, a teleprompter-style script card sliding in beside it, flat vector, no text."

### Scene 8: 14-day timeline (about 12 s)
- **Voiceover:** "After a full quality check, we set up your ad account and launch your ads. Start to finish, the project takes about fourteen days, and quick approvals from you keep it moving."
- **On-screen text:** "Live in about 14 days". A horizontal line drawing across: Onboarding → Strategy (3 days) → Your approval → Build (5 working days) → Quality check → Launch (emerald glow)
- **Grok Imagine prompt:** "A glowing horizontal timeline drawing itself left to right on a dark background, six nodes lighting up in sequence, the last node bursting into an emerald glow like a rocket launch, flat vector, no text."

### Scene 9: Questions (about 8 s)
- **Voiceover:** "Any question, at any point, just message us on your WhatsApp group. Now let us begin your onboarding."
- **On-screen text:** "Questions? Message us on your WhatsApp group" then "Let's begin"
